How I work

From first call to keys

  1. 01

    Getting to know you 30 minutes, free

    We talk through your income, credit history and the home you want. I tell you straight away whether approval is realistic and with which lenders.

  2. 02

    Documents 1–2 days

    I send you a checklist for your situation and check every document before we apply, so nothing is declined over a typo.

  3. 03

    Applications 2–5 days

    I get pre-approvals from 6–9 lenders at once. You don't have to visit branches or fill in the same form nine times.

  4. 04

    Choosing an offer 1–3 days

    I put the lenders' answers into one table: rate, payment, fees and total cost. You choose; I explain the differences.

  5. 05

    Closing until you get the keys

    I help with the appraisal, inspection, homeowners insurance and the title company, and stay in touch until closing and the keys are in your hand.

On average 5–9 days from the first call to lender decisions; the whole purchase takes 30–45 days to close

What to prepare.

Five groups of documents. I'll send the exact list for your situation after our first call

ID and address: what to bring
so the lender doesn't send it back

Personalchecklist · 4 items
  1. Driver’s license or passport for every borrower
  2. Proof of address from the last 3 months
  3. Marriage certificate or name-change documents, if relevant
  4. Details of anyone else who will live in the property

Proof of income: what to bring
so the lender doesn't send it back

Incomechecklist · 4 items
  1. Last 30 days of pay stubs and 2 years of W-2s
  2. Last 2 months of bank statements for your main account
  3. Self-employed: 2 years of tax returns and a year-to-date P&L
  4. Contractors: 1099s and your current contract

Property documents: what to bring
so the lender doesn't send it back

Propertychecklist · 4 items
  1. Signed purchase agreement
  2. Condo or HOA: dues, budget and HOA questionnaire
  3. Any inspection reports you already have
  4. New construction: builder contract and incentive details

Down payment: what to bring
so the lender doesn't send it back

Moneychecklist · 4 items
  1. Statements for the account holding your down payment
  2. A gift letter, if family is helping
  3. Down payment assistance approval, if you're using a program
  4. Closing statement if the down payment comes from selling a home

Credit history: what to bring
so the lender doesn't send it back

Checkchecklist · 4 items
  1. I run a soft credit pull with your consent; it doesn't affect your score
  2. I look at missed payments, existing debts and recent applications
  3. If you've cleared old debts, paid-in-full letters
  4. I tell you honestly which lenders to apply to with your history

Frequently asked
questions

What does a broker actually do?

I don't lend you money; I help you get it. Your mortgage contract is directly with the lender, and the funds go to the title company at closing, never to me.

Why not just go to my bank?

You can. But each hard credit pull shows on your report, and a run of declines lowers your chances with the next lender. I apply where approval is most likely.

I have a poor credit history. Do I have a chance?

It depends on the reason and how long ago it was. I'll check your credit report first and tell you honestly. Sometimes settling one old missed payment is enough.

How long does approval take?

Pre-approvals usually take 2–5 business days. Closing usually takes 30–45 days, depending on the property and the appraisal.

Can I get a mortgage if I'm self-employed?

Yes, many lenders accept bank statements or tax returns, some after just one year in business. Let's talk through your situation.

What if the lender approves me but not the property?

That's why I check the property before your inspection and appraisal deadlines. If there's a problem, it's better to know before your earnest money is at risk.

Do you only work in Denver?

I meet clients across the Denver metro in person and work remotely with buyers anywhere in Colorado.
